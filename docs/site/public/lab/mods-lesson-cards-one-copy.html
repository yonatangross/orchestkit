The lesson is drawn once, in the card

A block lesson used to print the same paragraph three times: in the red card under the command, again inside the Proceed anyway question, and again in the red deny line after Cancel or Escape. Now the card is the only copy. The question carries the lesson title only, and the deny is one short line with why, the lesson id and a short fix. Escape reads as cancelled by you. Claude Code still prefixes every mod deny with Error:; a mod cannot change that prefix.

lesson: git-ls-files-quotepath-blind  block
A "not tracked" read from `git ls-files` is blind to non-ASCII paths, ...
