What the public page scan bans and what it allows

Pages under docs/site/public/lab are public, and so are the playground sources they come from. A new test scans every one of them, plus every lab manifest fragment, and refuses a real home or desk path. Two pages that were already public are fixed here.

The card-only-mode page and its Lab copy quoted a desk folder as the listed folder. They now use /Users/you/lanes. The v10-ga data file named a desk screenshots folder. It now names ~/shots/orchestkit-v10-ga/. The scan covers every published page, so the next one is caught on its own PR.
