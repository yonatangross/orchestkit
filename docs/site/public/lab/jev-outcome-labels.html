ORCHESTKIT LAB · JEV OUTCOME LABELS

A weak label needs an evidence chain

Jev can recommend an executor in shadow mode. This review aid labels that recommendation only when structured events prove a relationship. A label is not a replacement executor choice and never changes a routed session.

Try synthetic evidence cases

Each case is a fixed example inside this page. No log, identifier, key, network request, or browser storage is read.

The examples show a weak outcome only. They do not expose or infer a corrected pick.

The conservative rules

EvidenceResultReason
Missing prompt identityunknownSession reuse is not enough to join a signal.
One exact primary handoff to another executorweak wrongAn unambiguous alternate contradicts the Jev executor.
Clean completed stop plus matching primary executionweak correctBoth the outcome and the executed handoff are explicit.
Bare stop or end turnunknownCompletion flags alone do not prove which executor ran.

Confirmed labels stay separate

A human reviewed outcome belongs in a separate confirmed-label store. Weak signals can sort a review queue, but cannot overwrite a confirmed outcome or manufacture a corrected recommendation.
