The guard stops multiplying homes.

Review found three more tilde-path divergences after the last round merged: HOME tilde candidates multiplied without bound on repeated assignments, an assignment list expanded later tildes against a stale home, and an elif condition holding && or || marked every following branch assignment conditional. Each was reproduced, verified against bash, and closed at the mechanism.
