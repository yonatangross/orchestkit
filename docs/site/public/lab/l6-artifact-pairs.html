Artifact pairs: plans and research you review in the file

Five ork skills port humanlayer's riptide create/iterate pattern. Each artifact is one file under .ork/tasks/<task>/. Review comments live at the end of that file, and an iterate rewrites the body but keeps every comment. Step through the life of one plan below.
