A new OrchestKit icon: the rose window conductor

The docs site showed a plain text "O" as its favicon and had no app icon. It now carries the icon picked on 2026-10-20 after eight design rounds: a stained-glass rose window with a conductor raising a baton. On light pages the dark tile drops away and the window itself is the edge (variant A, "cut-out window").
