Home hero and nav logo: before and after

The hero looked broken on laptops because the art was taken out of the grid flow, so the right column sat empty beside a copy column about 600 px taller than the art. The nav logo was a detailed 128 px raster shrunk to 22 px.

Hero root cause. .home-hero-art was position:absolute; top:0; right:0; width:55% with a fixed aspect ratio, inside a two-column grid whose second column held nothing. The copy column carried the host picker, search and links, so everything under the art was bare grid. The 1180 px container cap also left the right gutter unused at 1575 px and up. In light mode the opaque night-scene art, feathered on one side only, showed as a black slab.

Hero fix. The art is a grid item in column 2, vertically centred on the copy. Install is host-first: the copy column holds the host picker and one command box for the picked host, with its Copy button, docs link and Get started. Search, links and the proof row move to a full-width row under the hero. Both arts are cropped to the subject (the source was 44% black on the left, which pushed the conductor ~490 px from the headline at 2000 px) and the box stays in column 2. Light mode has its own art, a GPT Image 2 restyle of the dark one. The theme switch reveals the page in a circle from the toggle while the incoming art settles in.

Logo root cause and fix. george-badge.png (128 px, detailed husky, no alpha) rendered at 22 px with a warm ring beside a wordmark whose "Kit" had its own gradient. The failure was the crop, not George: the suit, ring and disc were squeezed in with him. The nav now shows an 84 px crop of his head at 30 px on a rounded square, so the ears, glasses and two eye colors read, beside a one-color wordmark.
