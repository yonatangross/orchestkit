The privacy page said local storage only, while the browser held a ph_ cookie

The docs site privacy page claimed browser storage was limited to a theme preference in local storage. The site loads posthog-js from instrumentation-client.ts, and the init options never set persistence. Measured against the installed posthog-js 1.430.2 build, the SDK default is persistence: "localStorage+cookie", so every visitor also gets a first-party cookie named ph_<project-key>_posthog. GH-4109 corrects the Cookies and tracking section to name that cookie instead of implying it does not exist.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not use advertising cookies or cross-site
trackers. Theme preference may be stored in your browser's
local storage purely to remember light or dark mode.

Read literally, the paragraph rules out any other browser storage. It was written before the PostHog mirror shipped and was never updated.

Cookies and tracking

The site does not use advertising cookies or cross-site trackers.
Theme preference may be stored in your browser's local storage
purely to remember light or dark mode. Analytics run through
PostHog, which also sets a first-party cookie whose name begins
with ph_ (of the form ph_<project-key>_posthog) holding only an
anonymous distinct id and a session id; that data stays with this
site's own analytics and is not shared with advertisers. The site
does not offer an opt-out or consent prompt for analytics;
blocking or clearing cookies that begin with ph_ in your browser
settings is the only way to decline them.

Names the cookie, says what it holds, repeats the no advertisers promise, and states plainly that no opt-out control exists. None was invented.

Three sources of truth, checked before writing the sentence

SourceWhat it says
instrumentation-client.ts init optionsSets api_host, ui_host, defaults, replay masking, person_profiles: "identified_only", capture_exceptions. No persistence key, so the SDK default applies.
posthog-js 1.430.2 installed buildDefault config ships persistence: "localStorage+cookie" and respect_dnt: false. The persistence key is "ph_" + (persistence_name || token) + "_posthog".
Consent surface on the siteNone. No cookie banner, no toggle, no do-not-track handling anywhere under app/, components/ or lib/. The corrected text says so instead of inventing one.
