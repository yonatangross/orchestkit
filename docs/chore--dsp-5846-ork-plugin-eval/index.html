02 · What the numbers say

  • Natural triggering is the gap. The with-arm lists ork:ci-debug, ork:review-pr, ork:fix-issue and ork:create-pr among its skills and never calls the Skill tool on prompts that do not name one.
  • No over-triggering. Every should-not-fire and control case held at 0 delta. The control cost gap (about $0.06 to $0.08 more per run) is the always-on context tax.
  • Two runs per arm is noise level. A re-run of case 72 reversed its result. Treat any single-case delta under about 30 points as unconfirmed.

03 · Caveats
