03 / THE CONTRACT (references/js-art.md) ─────────────────────────────────
  draw(ctx, t, mode)   pure: same t + mode, same pixels
  window.seek(t)       draws frame t, for capture to mp4 / gif
  window.setMode(m)    'now' | 'ideal', buttons use aria-pressed
  play()               requestAnimationFrame only when motion is allowed
  rng(seed)            seeded PRNG, Math.random is banned
  no network, no CDN, light paper colors, no em dash

04 / THREE PATTERNS ──────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  flow dots     items move on a path, speed and spacing are the message
  gate          items queue at a gate, open/shut cycle shows the wait
  before/after  same scene, two modes, one toggle (Now vs Ideal)
