The support window, after this bump

Three numbers describe how current OrchestKit is against Claude Code. The floor is the oldest CC the plugin vouches for (frozen by owner override until 2026-11-20). latest_known is the newest CC that triage has verified. upstream is what npm ships. The gap between the last two is the blind spot every alpha ships with. Drag upstream forward to see when the staleness alarm (threshold 2) fires again.

What each bump actually asserts

A latest_known bump says: every changelog bullet up to this version was checked against ork's hooks, settings, MCP config, agent frontmatter and docs, and anything that needed changing was changed or filed. It does not move the floor, so no user is dropped. A floor bump (the July renewals) says something stronger: ork stops vouching for older CC and can rely on newer behaviours. Both previous floor renewals were done at the head after a full sweep; this PR makes the next one possible without an open backlog.
